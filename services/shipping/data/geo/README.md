# Geo reference data

`communes.csv` holds the 1,541 communes, with their wilaya after the 2026 reform (law 26-06).

- Source: the GeoAlgeria compilation (npm `geoalgeria` 2.4.1, github.com/yasserstudio/geoalgeria), names, postal codes and ONS commune codes only.
- Licence: MIT, see `LICENSE.geoalgeria`. We do not copy its coordinates or boundaries, which are ODbL (OpenStreetMap), nor its mirrored Algérie Poste data.
- `aliases` are ours: extra spellings sellers type.

Details and the update procedure: `docs/data-sources.md`.
